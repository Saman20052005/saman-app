# Saman Fitness: Master Product Requirements Document (PRD) & Technical Design Specification (v3.6)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

**Saman** is an editorial, high-performance strength and conditioning mobile platform engineered specifically for dedicated trainees, lifters, and athletes.

Unlike conventional fitness applications that rely on hyperactive gamification, neon aesthetic gimmicks, cartoon avatars, exaggerated physiques, and disruptive social feeds, Saman is anchored in the foundational philosophy of **Calm Athleticism**. It provides a focused, distraction-free digital sanctuary combining stoic ergonomics, high-contrast typography, authentic sports documentary photography, and grounded biometric intelligence.

### 1.1 Core Value Proposition
The platform answers three fundamental questions for athletes in under 5 seconds:
1. **What can I train today?** (Immediate access to scheduled workouts and movement splits without navigation friction).
2. **How do I start?** (Frictionless one-tap initiation into structured sessions or free logging).
3. **Where can I see what I actually completed?** (Verifiable, chronological history of volume, duration, and exertion without fabricated vanity metrics).

## 2. Core Personas & User Journeys

### 2.1 Primary Persona: The Dedicated Strength Trainee ("The Methodical Lifter")
- **Demographics & Profile**: 22–45 years old, trains 3–6 days per week with barbells, dumbbells, cables, and bodyweight movements.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Overwhelmed by flashy social feeds, cartoon badges, and arbitrary "level-up" points in consumer apps.
  - Frustrated by navigation friction (often requiring 5+ taps just to begin logging a workout).
  - Distrustful of unverified metrics, fake caloric burn estimates, and cluttered telemetry.
- **Key Flow**:
  - Opens the app directly to the Workout tab.
  - Confirms today's scheduled split (e.g., *Upper Body Strength · 50 min · 5 exercises*).
  - Taps **"Start workout"** for immediate execution into the **Active Workout** logging flow.
  - Logs sets, adjusts weight/reps with comfortable touch-friendly steppers, and observes rest intervals.
  - Taps **"Finish"** to trigger the post-session review and commit the verified volume into history.

### 2.2 Secondary Persona: The Adaptive Guided Athlete
- **Demographics & Profile**: Trainee balancing structured strength work with recovery, mobility, and personalized nutrition targets.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Lack of cohesive feedback linking sleep quality, soreness, and lifting intensity.
  - Inflexible static routines that don't adapt to systemic fatigue.
- **Key Flow**:
  - Interacts with **Saman Coach** (multimodal conversational AI) to adjust intensity based on systemic fatigue and protein targets.
  - Reviews holistic metrics across training volume, dietary macros, and rest intervals.

### 2.3 Tertiary Flow: Real-Time Movement Correction ("Live Form Check")
- **Profile**: Athlete executing technical compound lifts (pull-ups, squats, deadlifts) seeking objective real-time postural guidance without hiring an in-person trainer.
- **Key Flow**:
  - Triggers **"Check my form"** from an Exercise Detail page or mid-set during an Active Workout session.
  - Places the mobile device 2–3 meters away, angling it to capture full body and apparatus (e.g. pull-up bar).
  - Receives live optical feedback: observed rep tally and a single, glanceable, high-contrast form cue (e.g., *"Keep shoulders down as you pull"*).
  - Taps **"End form check"** to return safely to the active session with zero destructive or premature logging.

## 3. Global Information Architecture & Navigation

Saman enforces a dual-mode navigation model:
1. **Persistent 5-Tab Navigation System** across exploration, coaching, nutrition, and profile hubs.
2. **Dedicated In-Session Operational Mode** during active training and live camera tracking, deliberately suppressing the global bottom tab bar to prevent accidental exits or destructive context switching while lifting.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Viewport Top: Safe-Area Context Header / Subpage Back Arrow |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Dynamic Scrollable Viewport (Smooth Inertial Scrolling)     |
|   - Minimum Content clearance: pb-28 / pb-32                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Fixed Bottom Navigation Bar (Glass Obsidian #0C0D0E / 95%)  |
| [1. Home] [2. Workout*] [3. Saman (S)] [4. Nutrition] [5. Profile] |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Top-Level Navigation Tabs
1. **Home**: Daily holistic summary (readiness telemetry, nutrition overview, adherence streak).
2. **Workout [Active Operational Core]**: Training launchpad, scheduled hero session, quick-start matrix, movement-focus exploration, forward horizon, and verified history.
3. **Saman Coach (Monogram `S`)**: Multimodal conversational AI coach providing workout and nutrition adjustments, RPE calibrations, and telemetry analysis.
4. **Nutrition**: Macronutrient tracking (protein, carbs, fats), meal logging, and hydration telemetry.
5. **Profile**: User biometric profile, primary goals, activity level, connected wearable integrations (Apple Health, Whoop), preferences, and account membership.

## 4. Screen-by-Screen Specifications & Design Contracts

### 4.1 Workout Tab (Main Operational Hub)
- **Header & Secondary Utility**:
  - Headline: `Workout` (`Space Grotesk`, 28px, Semibold).
  - Subtitle: `Train, log & explore movements` (`Inter`, 14px, muted slate).
  - Action Trigger: `My Plans` button (`#16171B` background, 1px border, bookmark icon).
- **Hero Module ("Today's Scheduled Session")**:
  - **Status Pill**: Understated green `Planned` chip (`#10B981` tint).
  - **Visual Asset**: Authentic sports documentary photography of an athlete performing an upper-body lift in a modern architectural gym with natural directional lighting.
  - **Text Contrast**: Targeted bottom gradient scrim ensuring WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1+) without murky full-image darkening.
  - **Protocol Tag & Title**: Bold session name (`Upper Body Strength`) with protocol tag (`Upper Body Protocol`).
  - **Essential Metrics**: Exactly two high-utility figures: Duration (`50 min`) and Movement Count (`5 exercises`).
  - **Action Pair**:
    - **Primary CTA**: Prominent, solid off-white (`#F9FAFB`) button with obsidian text (`#0C0D0E`): `Start workout`.
    - **Secondary CTA**: Ghost action: `View plan >`.
- **Quick Action Matrix**:
  - **Quick Start Card**: Frictionless logging for unstructured workouts (`Train without a plan`).
  - **Browse Library Card**: Direct gateway to movement discovery (`Browse library` / `Explore exercises`).
- **"Train by Focus" (Movement Categories)**:
  - Dual photo-led category cards:
    - **Upper Body**: Strict pulling/pressing imagery, labeled with muscle groups (`Chest, Back, Arms`).
    - **Lower Body**: Barbell squat imagery, labeled with muscle groups (`Quads, Glutes, Legs`).
    - *Policy*: Strictly movement-based categories; zero gender segregation or artificial restrictions.
- **"Your Plan" (Forward Horizon)**:
  - Minimalist preview showing 1–2 upcoming days to reduce cognitive load.
  - Displays untruncated session titles with plain duration badges (e.g., `Lower Body Strength · 45 min · 5 exercises` and `Active Recovery · 25 min flow · Mobility`).
- **"Last Workout" & History Verification**:
  - Displays the single most recent completed session (e.g., `Leg Day & Posterior Chain · Yesterday • 48 min • 5 exercises`) with a verified checkmark indicator.
  - *Dual-State Contract*: The populated card and empty-history state must never be shown simultaneously.
  - Persistent link to `View history >`.

  - Exercise title: `Dumbbell Bench Press` with zero unexplained icons or gamified medals.

### 4.4 Active Workout Execution Screen (Core Training Hub)
- **Design Philosophy**:
  - Replaces rigid dashboards and spreadsheet tables with an energetic, photo-anchored athletic training flow optimized for rapid operation between sets with sweaty or chalked hands.
  - Strict omission of the global 5-tab bottom navigation to prevent accidental exits.
- **Header & Session Control**:
  - **Minimize Control**: Discreet top-left chevron (`expand_more`) allowing trainees to pause or background the session safely.
  - **Session Title & Live Timer**: Centered `Upper Body Strength` with live elapsed chronometer (`18:42` with an understated emerald pulse).
  - **"Finish" Action**: Top-right quiet ghost button (`#16171B` container, hairline border). Tapping opens a future **Review & Save Summary** step; it **never** silently marks the session as finished or saved.
- **Progress Horizon**:
  - Compact single-line telemetry: `Exercise 1 of 5` and `2 of 4 sets completed`, supported by an ultra-thin 5-segment horizon line.
- **Current Exercise Anchor**:
  - Full-width documentary photograph of the athlete executing the Dumbbell Bench Press.
  - On-image frosted glass pill: **`▶ Video Guide`** positioned unobtrusively in the lower right corner, returning to form cues without losing session state.
  - Bold headline: `Dumbbell Bench Press` with single equipment subtitle: `Dumbbells · Flat bench`.
- **Contextual Rest Interval Strip**:
  - Positioned immediately beneath the exercise heading so it **never covers editable weight or rep fields**.
  - Displays remaining countdown: `REST 01:14` with emerald progress ring, accompanied by tactile `+30s` and `Skip` chip actions.
- **Ergonomic Set-Logging Matrix**:
  - **Sets 1 & 2 (Completed)**: Soft divider rows showing illustrative recorded volume (`24 kg × 10`, `26 kg × 8`) and restrained emerald checkmark badges (`#10B981`).
  - **Set 3 (Active / In-Progress)**: Visually distinct elevated card (`#1E2026`) without harsh neon borders. Features large, tactile 48px+ stepper controls (`−` and `+`) separated from high-contrast numerical displays (`26 kg` and `8 reps`).
  - **Single Action Contract**: The active set row does **not** contain a second competing checkbox. Entering or adjusting values does not record the set; only tapping the bottom primary button records the set.
  - **Set 4 (Upcoming / Planned)**: Muted values (`26 kg × 8 —`) indicating planned work, clearly pending and not recorded.
  - **"Add set" Action**: Compact dashed trigger beneath the matrix allowing lifters to dynamically add sets without rigid constraints.
- **Secondary Next Movement & Library Access**:
  - Compact preview: `Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip) · 4 sets · Cable Machine`.
  - Secondary text link: `View all 5 exercises →`.
- **Grounded Action Dock**:
  - Fixed above the device safe area.
  - **Primary CTA**: Prominent, full-width solid off-white button (`#F9FAFB`) with dark obsidian text (`#0C0D0E`): **`✓ Log Set 3`**. Tapping records the active set and automatically triggers the next rest countdown.
  - **Secondary Utility**: Discreet text trigger centered beneath the button: `⏸ Pause workout`.

### 4.5 Live Form Check (Optical Computer Vision Hub — Wide Grip Pull-up)
- **Design Philosophy**:
  - Acts as a focused, stoic training mirror rather than a sci-fi surveillance interface.
  - Zero neon skeletons, glowing bone heatmaps, complex joint angles, or speculative precision percentages.
  - True edge-to-edge full-bleed portrait mobile UI (~390 × 844) designed to be readable from 2–4 meters away while moving.
- **Navigation & Header Architecture**:
  - Compact top header with discreet close button (`close`), active tracking status badge (`● FORM CHECK` / `● TRACKING`), and exercise title (`Wide Grip Pull-up`).
  - Global 5-tab bottom navigation is **deliberately suppressed** during camera operation.
  - Audio/speaker control is omitted in the visual-only design.
- **Camera Viewport & Environmental Framing**:
  - Live camera occupies 80%+ of the vertical mobile screen.
  - Anchored by floor-angled sports documentary photography capturing the lifter, the pull-up bar, and full vertical extension (head to feet) without letterboxing or bezel embedding.
  - Visual safe zone: HUD elements are docked to top/bottom screen perimeters, keeping the lifter's face, hands, bar grip, and feet completely unobstructed.
- **Camera Setup & Placement Guidance**:
  - Subdued frosted glass pill positioned directly beneath the header: *"Keep your full body and the bar in view"*.
  - Plain tracking state badge: `● Full body in frame`.
- **Distance-Readable Telemetry HUD**:
  - **Observed Rep Counter**: Located at the lower-left edge of the preview frame: high-contrast tabular readout with uppercase label `REPS OBSERVED` / `LIVE OBSERVATION`.
  - *Data Contract*: Observed reps are strictly transient computer-vision metrics; they are **never** committed automatically to the user's workout log.
- **Actionable Form Cue Card & Multi-State Feedback System**:
  - High-visibility card docked beneath the lifter's movement field.
  - Strict rule: Exactly **one** concise, actionable cue is shown at a time. No competing positive badges or multi-sentence paragraphs during active movement correction.
  - Communicates meaning through distinct semantic iconography, micro-caps classification tags, and color—never through color alone.
- **The Four Canonical State Variants**:
  1. **State A (Form Correction)**:
     - *Header Status*: `● FORM CHECK` in restrained emerald.
     - *Rep Counter*: `4 REPS OBSERVED`.
     - *Feedback Card*: Calm Fuel Amber (`#F59E0B`) container with downward arrow icon and `ADJUST FORM` tag: *"Keep your shoulders down on the next rep."* Avoids blame, aggressive red alerts, or injury claims.
  2. **State B (Positive Confirmation)**:
     - *Header Status*: `● TRACKING` in restrained emerald.
     - *Rep Counter*: Advances to `5 REPS OBSERVED`.
     - *Feedback Card*: Restrained emerald checkmark icon (`#10B981`) with `REP COMPLETE` tag: *"Good rep — chin cleared the bar."* Focused, brief positive reinforcement that clears naturally.
  3. **State C (Reminder Between Reps)**:
     - *Header Status*: `● TRACKING` in restrained emerald.
     - *Rep Counter*: `4 REPS OBSERVED` held steady.
     - *Feedback Card*: Neutral obsidian card with tempo/timer icon and `BETWEEN REPS` tag: *"Lower with control before your next pull."* Acts as a calm coaching cue for the eccentric phase.
  4. **State D (Tracking Interrupted)**:
     - *Header Status*: Switches cleanly to `● TRACKING PAUSED` in amber micro-caps.
     - *Rep Counter*: Visually frozen counter (`4 REPS HELD · FROZEN`) so athletes know counting is held rather than lost or erroneously incrementing.
     - *Feedback Card*: Replaces form judgment with camera placement guidance, featuring an athlete detection icon and `PLACEMENT GUIDANCE` tag: *"Tracking paused — move back until your full body and the bar are visible."* Zero positive or negative judgment shown while tracking is uncertain.
     - *Action Dock Utility*: Adapts to `▶ Resume camera`.
- **Bottom Action Dock**:
  - Full-width, solid warm off-white (`#F9FAFB`) primary button: **`End form check`** with dark obsidian text (`#0C0D0E`).
  - Secondary utility centered beneath: **`⏸ Pause camera`** (or `▶ Resume camera` when paused).
  - Non-destructive exit: Tapping "End form check" returns the trainee safely to the originating Active Workout or Exercise Detail screen, allowing them to manually confirm or discard reps.

## 5. Visual Design System: "Calm Athleticism"

### 5.1 Color Architecture
| Token Name | Hex Value | Semantic Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Canvas Base** | `#0C0D0E` | Root canvas background, screen borders, modal backdrops |
| **Surface Low** | `#121316` | Secondary containers, bottom navigation background |
| **Surface Card** | `#16171B` | Primary interactive cards, panels, exercise rows |
| **Surface High** | `#1E2026` / `#1F2126` | Active set containers, stepper inputs, pill badges |
| **Hairline Border** | `#26282F` / `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` | 1px structural dividing lines |
| **Text Primary** | `#F9FAFB` | Headlines, primary CTA background, critical numbers |
| **Text Slate** | `#CBD5E1` / `#9CA3AF` | Subtitles, duration badges, exercise counts |
| **Text Muted** | `#6B7280` / `#86948A` | Column headers, micro caps, inactive navigation icons |
| **Performance Green** | `#10B981` | Completed checks, active streaks, planned status, live tracking active |
| **Fuel Amber** | `#F59E0B` | Moderate exertion cautions, form adjustment cues, paused tracking state |
| **Metabolic Violet**| `#A855F7` | Recovery status, fat macronutrients |

### 5.2 Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Architectural precision without athletic cliché. Used for screen titles (28px), card headlines (18–22px), and section headers.
- **Body & Interface (`Inter`)**: High-legibility workhorse (13–16px) for workout descriptions, list rows, form cues, and navigation labels.
- **Telemetry & Micro-Caps (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for durations, weights, exercise counts, reps observed, dates, and uppercase micro section headers (`0.12em` letter spacing).

### 5.3 Elevation & Contour Philosophy
- **Zero Ambient Drop Shadows**: Depth is established exclusively through **Tonal Stratification** (layering darker and lighter obsidian surfaces) and **1px Hairline Borders**.
- **Machined Radii**:
  - Buttons, chips, and small controls: `4px` (`rounded-sm`).
  - Cards, panels, and exercise rows: `12px–16px` (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`).

## 6. Non-Functional, Ergonomic & Usability Standards

1. **Strict Viewport Anchoring & iOS Ergonomics**:
   - The 5-tab bottom navigation remains permanently anchored during standard browsing.
   - During Active Workout and Live Form Check, bottom tab navigation is replaced with dedicated in-session action docks (`Log Set` / `End form check` + secondary utilities).
   - Dynamic content scrolls smoothly with a minimum clearance of `pb-28` to `pb-32` so active inputs or secondary links are never obscured.
2. **Narrow Viewport Resilience (320px–430px)**:
   - Text containers must never clip or truncate critical titles. Multi-line wrapping and concise plain-language descriptors are enforced down to 320px viewports.
   - Active steppers, camera controls, and buttons enforce a minimum 44px–48px touch target for gym floor usability.
3. **Data Integrity & Grounding**:
   - Zero hallucinated or arbitrary counts (no unverified personal records, arbitrary calorie counts, or ungrounded counts like "300+ movements").
   - Camera-based observed reps remain strictly observational until verified and manually accepted by the lifter.
4. **Authentic Sports Documentary Imagery**:
   - Authentic sports photography showing realistic anatomy, proper lifting form, and visible equipment. Zero cartoon avatars, hyper-muscular exaggerations, glowing sci-fi HUDs, or distracting graphics.
