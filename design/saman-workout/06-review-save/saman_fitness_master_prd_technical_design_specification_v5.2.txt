# Saman Fitness: Master Product Requirements Document (PRD) & Technical Design Specification (v5.2)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

**Saman** is an editorial, high-performance strength, conditioning, and nutrition mobile platform engineered specifically for dedicated trainees, lifters, and athletes.

Unlike conventional fitness applications that rely on hyperactive gamification, neon aesthetic gimmicks, cartoon avatars, exaggerated physiques, and disruptive social feeds, Saman is anchored in the foundational philosophy of **Calm Athleticism**. It provides a focused, distraction-free digital sanctuary combining stoic ergonomics, high-contrast typography, authentic sports and culinary documentary photography, and grounded biometric intelligence.

### 1.1 Core Value Proposition
The platform answers four fundamental questions for athletes in under 5 seconds:
1. **What can I train today?** (Immediate access to scheduled workouts and movement splits without navigation friction).
2. **How do I start?** (Frictionless one-tap initiation into structured sessions or free logging).
3. **Where can I see what I actually completed?** (Verifiable, chronological history of volume, duration, and exertion without fabricated vanity metrics).
4. **How am I fueling today's performance?** (Clear distinction between confirmed dietary intake and planned meals, frictionless search and photo meal logging, and precision macro/hydration telemetry).

## 2. Core Personas & User Journeys

### 2.1 Primary Persona: The Dedicated Strength Trainee ("The Methodical Lifter")
- **Demographics & Profile**: 22–45 years old, trains 3–6 days per week with barbells, dumbbells, cables, and bodyweight movements.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Overwhelmed by flashy social feeds, cartoon badges, and arbitrary "level-up" points in consumer apps.
  - Frustrated by navigation friction (often requiring 5+ taps just to begin logging a workout or meal).
  - Distrustful of unverified metrics, fake caloric burn estimates, and cluttered telemetry.
- **Key Training Flow**:
  - Opens the app directly to the Workout tab.
  - Confirms today's scheduled split (e.g., *Upper Body Strength · 50 min · 5 exercises*).
  - Taps **"Start workout"** for immediate execution into the **Active Workout** logging flow.
  - Logs sets, adjusts weight/reps with comfortable touch-friendly steppers, and observes rest intervals.
  - Taps **"Finish"** to trigger the post-session review and commit the verified volume into history.

### 2.2 Secondary Persona: The Adaptive Guided Athlete
- **Demographics & Profile**: Trainee balancing structured strength work with recovery, mobility, and personalized nutrition targets.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Lack of cohesive feedback linking sleep quality, soreness, and lifting intensity.
  - Inflexible static routines that don't adapt to systemic fatigue.
- **Key Coaching Flow**:
  - Interacts with **Saman Coach** (multimodal conversational AI) to adjust intensity based on systemic fatigue and protein targets.
  - Reviews holistic metrics across training volume, dietary macros, and rest intervals.

### 2.3 Tertiary Persona: The Precision Fueler ("The Nutrition Tracker")
- **Demographics & Profile**: Lifter tracking daily energy balance, macronutrient distribution, and hydration to optimize hypertrophy and recovery.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Apps that conflate planned meals with actual food consumed, corrupting daily totals.
  - Slow, multi-step food logging workflows with noisy search databases.
  - Arbitrary visual gauges that obscure the core relationship between consumed calories and daily targets.
- **Key Nutrition Flow**:
  - Navigates to the **Nutrition** tab to verify today's confirmed intake vs. target.
  - Quickly logs meals via high-contrast **"Log meal"** (catalog/search) or **"Scan food"** (multimodal vision capture).
  - Filters by meal slots (`Lunch`, `Dinner`) and queries foods with clean caloric and reference-portion metadata.
  - Increments hydration via a tactile **"+250 ml"** action, observing a discrete 8-segment rhythmic meter.
  - Safely inspects the upcoming planned meal and triggers **"Review & log"** before confirming ingestion.

### 2.4 Quaternary Flow: Real-Time Movement Correction ("Live Form Check")
- **Profile**: Athlete executing technical compound lifts (pull-ups, squats, deadlifts) seeking objective real-time postural guidance without hiring an in-person trainer.
- **Key Flow**:
  - Triggers **"Check my form"** from an Exercise Detail page or mid-set during an Active Workout session.
  - Places the mobile device 2–3 meters away, angling it to capture full body and apparatus (e.g. pull-up bar).
  - Receives live optical feedback: observed rep tally and a single, glanceable, high-contrast form cue (e.g., *"Keep shoulders down as you pull"*).
  - Taps **"End form check"** to return safely to the active session with zero destructive or premature logging.

## 3. Global Information Architecture & Navigation

Saman enforces a dual-mode navigation model:
1. **Persistent 5-Tab Navigation System** across exploration, coaching, nutrition, and profile hubs.
2. **Dedicated In-Session Operational Mode** during active training, post-workout review, workout complete checkpoint, and live camera tracking, deliberately suppressing the global bottom tab bar to prevent accidental exits or destructive context switching while lifting.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Viewport Top: Safe-Area Context Header / Subpage Back Arrow |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Dynamic Scrollable Viewport (Smooth Inertial Scrolling)     |
|   - Minimum Content clearance: pb-28 / pb-36                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Fixed Bottom Navigation Bar (Glass Obsidian #0C0D0E / 95%)  |
| [1. Home] [2. Workout] [3. Saman (S)] [4. Nutrition] [5. Profile] |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Top-Level Navigation Tabs
1. **Home**: Daily holistic summary (readiness telemetry, nutrition overview, adherence streak).
2. **Workout [Operational Training Core]**: Training launchpad, scheduled hero session, quick-start matrix, movement-focus exploration, forward horizon, and verified history.
3. **Saman Coach (Monogram `S`)**: Multimodal conversational AI coach providing workout and nutrition adjustments, RPE calibrations, and telemetry analysis.
4. **Nutrition [Operational Fueling Core]**: Energy balance, macronutrient distribution, verified logged meals, segmented hydration meter, and planned meal previews.
5. **Profile**: User biometric profile, primary goals, activity level, connected wearable integrations (Apple Health, Whoop), preferences, and account membership.

### 3.2 Fixed Bottom Navigation Standard (The Approved 5-Tab Bar)
- **Container Structure**: Fixed at `bottom-0 left-0 right-0`, `z-50`, with `pb-safe` (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), flat obsidian ground `#0C0D0E/95` with `backdrop-blur-xl`, and a 1px top border `#26282F`.
- **Dimensions & Touch Targets**: Standard 64px content height (`max-w-[430px]`), five evenly spaced touch targets (minimum `56 × 44 px`).
- **Icons & Typography**:
  - Icons: Material Symbols Outlined, 20px.
  - Labels: JetBrains Mono, 9px, uppercase, tracking-wider (`0.12em`).
  - Active Tab: High-contrast white `#F9FAFB` icon and label with a centered 4px circular micro-dot indicator below.
  - Inactive Tabs: Readable muted slate `#9CA3AF` (never near-black or unreadable).
- **Center Element**: Flat architectural monogram **"S"** (`Space Grotesk`, bold, 18px), aligned cleanly in the same row without raised circular buttons, drop shadows, or glow effects.

### 4.4 Active Workout Execution Screen (Core Training Hub)
- **Design Philosophy**:
  - Replaces rigid dashboards and spreadsheet tables with an energetic, photo-anchored athletic training flow optimized for rapid operation between sets with sweaty or chalked hands.
  - Strict omission of the global 5-tab bottom navigation to prevent accidental exits.
- **Header & Session Control**:
  - **Minimize Control**: Discreet top-left chevron (`expand_more`) allowing trainees to pause or background the session safely.
  - **Session Title & Live Timer**: Centered `Upper Body Strength` with live elapsed chronometer (`18:42` with an understated emerald pulse).
  - **"Finish" Action**: Top-right quiet ghost button (`#16171B` container, hairline border). Tapping opens a future **Review & Save Summary** step; it **never** silently marks the session as finished or saved.
- **Progress Horizon**:
  - Compact single-line telemetry: `Exercise 1 of 5` and `2 of 4 sets completed`, supported by an ultra-thin 5-segment horizon line.
- **Current Exercise Anchor**:
  - Full-width documentary photograph of the athlete executing the Dumbbell Bench Press.
  - On-image frosted glass pill: **`▶ Video Guide`** positioned unobtrusively in the lower right corner, returning to form cues without losing session state.
  - Bold headline: `Dumbbell Bench Press` with single equipment subtitle: `Dumbbells · Flat bench`.
- **Contextual Rest Interval Strip**:
  - Positioned immediately beneath the exercise heading so it **never covers editable weight or rep fields**.
  - Displays remaining countdown: `REST 01:14` with emerald progress ring, accompanied by tactile `+30s` and `Skip` chip actions.
- **Ergonomic Set-Logging Matrix**:
  - **Sets 1 & 2 (Completed)**: Soft divider rows showing illustrative recorded volume (`24 kg × 10`, `26 kg × 8`) and restrained emerald checkmark badges (`#10B981`).
  - **Set 3 (Active / In-Progress)**: Visually distinct elevated card (`#1E2026`) without harsh neon borders. Features large, tactile 48px+ stepper controls (`−` and `+`) separated from high-contrast numerical displays (`26 kg` and `8 reps`).
  - **Single Action Contract**: The active set row does **not** contain a second competing checkbox. Entering or adjusting values does not record the set; only tapping the bottom primary button records the set.
  - **Set 4 (Upcoming / Planned)**: Muted values (`26 kg × 8 —`) indicating planned work, clearly pending and not recorded.
  - **"Add set" Action**: Compact dashed trigger beneath the matrix allowing lifters to dynamically add sets without rigid constraints.
- **Secondary Next Movement & Library Access**:
  - Compact preview: `Lat Pulldown (Wide Grip) · 4 sets · Cable Machine`.
  - Secondary text link: `View all 5 exercises →`.
- **Grounded Action Dock**:
  - Fixed above the device safe area.
  - **Primary CTA**: Prominent, full-width solid off-white button (`#F9FAFB`) with dark obsidian text (`#0C0D0E`): **`✓ Log Set 3`**. Tapping records the active set and automatically triggers the next rest countdown.
  - **Secondary Utility**: Discreet text trigger centered beneath the button: `⏸ Pause workout`.

### 4.5 Post-Workout Review & Save Screen (Pre-Save Editorial Verification)
- **Screen Role & Flow Placement**:
  - The pre-save verification checkpoint presented immediately when an athlete taps "Finish" during an active workout session, **before** the session is committed or written to local storage.
  - Positioning: $\text{Active Workout Logging} \xrightarrow{\text{Finish}} \mathbf{\text{Review \& Save (Standalone Route)}} \xrightarrow{\text{Save session (success)}} \text{Workout Complete}$.
- **Pre-Save Contract & Wording**:
  - Sets have been recorded during the workout but have **not yet been committed** to history.
  - The status is strictly **Pre-Save / Unsaved** (`● STAGE · PENDING CONFIRMATION`); this screen never displays labels such as *"Completed"*, *"Session saved"*, *"Saved on this device"*, or *"100% verified"*.
- **Visual Architecture & Journal Hierarchy**:
  - **Compact Header**: Header back chevron (`< Review session`) with context pill `v2.4 LOCAL`.
  - **Photographic Banner**: Tasteful, shallow atmospheric photo banner showing an authentic lifter resting between sets in natural gym lighting (`#16171B` container ground).
  - **Session Telemetry Summary**:
    - Primary hero metric: **`1,078`** `kg` net accumulated volume.
    - Active duration companion: **`32`** `min`.
    - Factual supporting row: `4 sets logged · 2 movements` (`+4.2% vs avg`).
  - **Recorded Movements Journal**:
    - **Dumbbell Bench Press** (`448 kg total`):
      - Set 1: `24 kg × 10 reps = 240 kg`
      - Set 2: `26 kg × 8 reps = 208 kg`
    - **Lat Pulldown** (`630 kg total`):
      - Set 1: `35 kg × 10 reps = 350 kg`
      - Set 2: `35 kg × 8 reps = 280 kg`
  - **Pre-Save Local Storage Notice**:
    - Plain-language notice in lower container: *"This demo saves your workout on this device until the app restarts."*
- **Action Dock & Interaction States**:
  - **Primary Action**: **`Save session →`** (48px tactile rounded-xl button, `#F9FAFB` fill, `#0C0D0E` text).
  - **Secondary Action**: Accessible text link **`Keep training`**. Both back and "Keep training" safely return the athlete to the active session with all logged sets intact.
  - **Zero Sets Logged**: Button renders disabled (`opacity-40 cursor-not-allowed`) with supporting guidance *"At least 1 completed set required to save"*.
  - **Saving In-Progress**: Spinner engages, button label changes to *"Saving session..."*, and UI is locked against double-commits.
  - **Save Interrupted (Retry State)**: Screen remains open with alert *"Local save write interrupted"* and prominent **Retry** button.
  - **Success Route Transition**: Navigates forward to the standalone **Workout Complete** screen.

### 4.6 Workout Complete Screen (Post-Commit Celebration Checkpoint)
- **Screen Role & Flow Placement**:
  - The rewarding, athletic post-workout checkpoint presented to the trainee **strictly after** an active workout session has been successfully verified and committed into local persistence.
  - Positioning: $\text{Review \& Save} \xrightarrow{\text{Save session (success)}} \mathbf{\text{Workout Complete (Standalone Route)}} \xrightarrow{\text{View workout history / Back}} \text{Workout History / Workout Hub}$.
- **Architectural Contract**:
  - Represents a confirmed, committed historical event.
  - Displays strictly factual data recorded during the workout session with zero speculative vanity metrics (no calories, fake PR badges, heart rate telemetry, or false cloud-sync claims).
- **Visual Layout Hierarchy (Top to Bottom)**:
  1. **Compact Native Top App Bar**:
     - Single clean header row (`44px` height) with native iOS status bar space (`pt-safe`).
     - Ergonomic back action: `< Workout` (`Inter`, 14px, muted slate `#9CA3AF`).
     - Status badge: Subdued emerald chip `● SESSION COMPLETE` (`JetBrains Mono`, 10px uppercase, `#10B981` tint).
     - *Zero clutter*: Redundant headers and profile buttons are omitted.
  2. **Cinematic Photographic Hero (205px Fixed Height)**:
     - Authentic sports documentary photography featuring a male athlete performing flat dumbbell bench press in natural gym daylight with matte black dumbbells.
     - Robust container background fallback (`#16171B`) ensuring zero layout collapse if the image fails.
     - Directional dark obsidian gradient scrim (`#0C0D0E`) ensuring WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1+).
     - Inset context: Date/time badge (`Oct 7, 2026 · 10:36 AM`), primary headline **`Workout complete`** (`Space Grotesk`, 26px, Bold), session split `Upper Body Strength`, and calm coaching feedback: *"A solid session in the books."*
  3. **Session Telemetry Summary**:
     - Hero metric: **`1,078`** `kg lifted` (`Space Grotesk`, 32px, bold emerald `#10B981`).
     - Active duration companion: **`32`** `min` (`Space Grotesk`, 32px, bold off-white `#F9FAFB`).
     - Factual supporting row: `4 sets logged · 2 exercises performed` (`Inter`, 13px, `#9CA3AF`).
  4. **Logged Exercises Journal**:
     - **Dumbbell Bench Press** (`448 kg total`): Set 1 (`24 kg × 10 reps = 240 kg`), Set 2 (`26 kg × 8 reps = 208 kg`).
     - **Lat Pulldown** (`630 kg total`): Set 1 (`35 kg × 10 reps = 350 kg`), Set 2 (`35 kg × 8 reps = 280 kg`).
     - Tabular figures (`JetBrains Mono`, `tabular-nums`) with non-wrapping layout guaranteeing zero digit splitting or truncation.
  5. **Volume by Exercise Comparison Bar**:
     - Rendered with **muted performance teal** (`#14B8A6` / `#2DD4BF`) harmonizing with Saman's emerald palette (`#10B981`).
     - Multi-modal clarity:
       - **Dumbbell Bench Press**: Emerald `#10B981` — `448 kg (41.6%)`
       - **Lat Pulldown**: Muted Teal `#14B8A6` — `630 kg (58.4%)`
  6. **Local-Demo Storage Notice**:
     - Plain-language text in lower container: *"Saved on this device for this demo. It clears when the app restarts."*
  7. **Persistent Safe-Area Action Dock**:

  - *Missing Duration*: Displays a neutral dash (`—`) rather than misleading "0m".
  - *Single Logged Exercise*: Volume comparison bar renders as solid 100% emerald bar with zero layout shift.

### 4.7 Live Form Check (Optical Computer Vision Hub — Wide Grip Pull-up)
- **Design Philosophy**:
  - Acts as a focused, stoic training mirror rather than a sci-fi surveillance interface.
  - Zero neon skeletons, glowing bone heatmaps, complex joint angles, or speculative precision percentages.
  - True edge-to-edge full-bleed portrait mobile UI (~390 × 844) designed to be readable from 2–4 meters away while moving.
- **Navigation & Header Architecture**:
  - Compact top header with discreet close button (`close`), active tracking status badge (`● FORM CHECK` / `● TRACKING`), and exercise title (`Wide Grip Pull-up`).
  - Global 5-tab bottom navigation is **deliberately suppressed** during camera operation.
- **Camera Viewport & Environmental Framing**:
  - Live camera occupies 80%+ of the vertical mobile screen.
  - Anchored by floor-angled sports documentary photography capturing the lifter, the pull-up bar, and full vertical extension (head to feet) without letterboxing or bezel embedding.
- **The Four Canonical State Variants**:
  1. **State A (Form Correction)**: Calm Fuel Amber (`#F59E0B`) card with downward arrow icon: *"Keep your shoulders down on the next rep."*
  2. **State B (Positive Confirmation)**: Restrained emerald checkmark (`#10B981`) with `REP COMPLETE`: *"Good rep — chin cleared the bar."*
  3. **State C (Reminder Between Reps)**: Neutral card with tempo icon: *"Lower with control before your next pull."*
  4. **State D (Tracking Interrupted)**: Amber status `● TRACKING PAUSED`, frozen counter, placement guidance: *"Tracking paused — move back until your full body and the bar are visible."* Action dock updates to `▶ Resume camera`.
- **Bottom Action Dock**: Full-width solid off-white button: **`End form check`** with secondary `⏸ Pause camera` utility.

### 4.10 Saman Coach (Conversational Multimodal AI Hub)
- **Header & Navigation**:
  - Monogram `S` badge with `Saman Coach` title, side drawer toggle (`notes`), new chat trigger, and notification shortcuts.
  - Slide-out coaching drawer featuring shortcuts (`Weekly review`, `Nutrition review`, `Workout analysis`), recent conversations archive, active persona parameters, and schedule reminders.
- **Conversation Interface**:
  - High-contrast chat bubble architecture distinguishing athlete inquiries from structured AI responses.
  - **Direct Coaching Tone**: Analytical, cue-focused, concise, and grounded in biological telemetry (RPE targets, protein deltas).
  - **Structured Telemetry Tables**: Metric vs. Today vs. Target comparisons (Calories 2,280 / 2,400 kcal; Protein 156g / 180g; Workout status; Recovery state).

### 5.2 Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Architectural precision without athletic cliché. Used for screen titles (28px), card headlines (18–22px), and section headers.
- **Body & Interface (`Inter`)**: High-legibility workhorse (13–16px) for workout descriptions, food titles, list rows, form cues, and navigation labels.
- **Telemetry & Micro-Caps (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) for durations, weights, exercise counts, calories, macros, reps observed, dates, and uppercase micro section headers (`0.12em` letter spacing).

### 5.3 Elevation & Contour Philosophy
- **Zero Ambient Drop Shadows**: Depth is established exclusively through **Tonal Stratification** (layering darker and lighter obsidian surfaces) and **1px Hairline Borders**.
- **Machined Radii**:
  - Buttons, chips, and small controls: `4px` (`rounded-sm`).
  - Cards, panels, food rows, and exercise containers: `12px–16px` (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`).

## 6. Mathematical Grounding & Telemetry Integrity

All numbers across workouts and nutrition represent strict arithmetic consistency without speculative vanity numbers:

### 6.1 Workout Volume & Telemetry Arithmetic
$$\begin{aligned}
\text{Dumbbell Bench Press Set 1:} \quad & 24\text{ kg} \times 10\text{ reps} = 240\text{ kg} \\
\text{Dumbbell Bench Press Set 2:} \quad & 26\text{ kg} \times 8\text{ reps} = 208\text{ kg} \\
\mathbf{\text{DB Bench Press Total:}} \quad & \mathbf{448\text{ kg total}} \quad \left(\frac{448}{1078} = 41.56\% \approx 41.6\%\right) \\[6pt]
\text{Lat Pulldown Set 1:} \quad & 35\text{ kg} \times 10\text{ reps} = 350\text{ kg} \\
\text{Lat Pulldown Set 2:} \quad & 35\text{ kg} \times 8\text{ reps} = 280\text{ kg} \\
\mathbf{\text{Lat Pulldown Total:}} \quad & \mathbf{630\text{ kg total}} \quad \left(\frac{630}{1078} = 58.44\% \approx 58.4\%\right) \\[6pt]
\mathbf{\text{Net Accumulated Volume:}} \quad & \mathbf{1,078\text{ kg}} \quad (240 + 208 + 350 + 280)
\end{aligned}$$
- **Active Duration**: `32 min`
- **Logged Sets**: `4 sets logged`
- **Movements Performed**: `2 exercises`

## 7. State Resilience & Edge Cases (Specification Matrix)

| State Key | Trigger Condition | Visual & Behavioral Treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **State 1: Populated (Active)** | Normal user data loaded. | Full continuous calorie track, 3 macro micro-tiles, logged meals list, 8-segment water meter, and separated meal plan preview. |
| **State 2: Loading Skeleton** | Asynchronous network fetch. | Shimmering `#16171B` wireframes replacing text rows and thumbnails; interactive CTAs disabled; layout dimensions preserved to avoid layout shifts. |
| **State 3: No Logged Meals** | Trainee has logged 0 meals today. | Today's intake shows `0 / 2,300 kcal` with empty rail; empty state illustration in meals slot: *"No meals logged today. Tap '+ Log meal' or 'Scan food' to begin."* |
| **State 4: No Search Results** | Search query yields 0 database matches. | Quiet empty state card: *"No dishes found for '[query]'. Tap 'Create custom food' below to add it."* |
| **State 5: Zero Sets on Review** | Lifter taps Finish with 0 logged sets. | "Save session" button is disabled (`opacity-40 cursor-not-allowed`) with guidance *"At least 1 completed set required to save"*. |
| **State 6: Save Interrupted** | Local write error on session commit. | Review screen remains open; displays amber alert: *"Local save write interrupted"* with prominent **Retry** button. |
| **State 7: Single Exercise Session** | Lifter logs sets for only 1 exercise. | "Volume by exercise" bar renders 100% solid emerald with single exercise name and total kg; zero split layout shift. |
| **State 8: Missing Session Duration** | Workout chronometer interrupted/null. | Duration displays neutral dash (`—`) rather than misleading "0m". |
