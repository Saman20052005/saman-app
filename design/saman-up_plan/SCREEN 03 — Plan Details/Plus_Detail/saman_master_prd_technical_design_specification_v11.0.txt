# Saman Fitness: Master Product Requirements Document (PRD) & Technical Design Specification (v11.0)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision

**Saman** is an editorial, workout-first, AI-augmented strength and conditioning mobile companion engineered specifically for dedicated trainees, lifters, and performance athletes.

In deliberate contrast to conventional consumer fitness applications that subject users to hyperactive gamification, neon aesthetic gimmicks, cartoon avatars, unsolicited community feeds, and high cognitive load before a lift, Saman is anchored in the foundational philosophy of **Calm Athleticism**. It provides a focused digital sanctuary pairing stoic ergonomics, high-contrast typography, authentic sports documentary photography, and mathematically grounded biometric telemetry.

### 1.1 The 5-Second Athlete Contract
The app answers three fundamental questions for an athlete in under 5 seconds:
1. **What can I train today?** (Immediate access to today's scheduled workout hero and movement splits without navigation friction).
2. **How do I start?** (Frictionless one-tap initiation into structured sessions via `Start workout` or unstructured tracking via `Quick start`).
3. **Where can I see what I actually completed?** (Verifiable, chronological history of volume, duration, and exertion without vanity metrics or fabricated estimates).

## 2. Core Personas & Problem Space

### 2.1 The Core Problems
1. **Dashboard & Metric Fatigue:** Modern fitness apps present disconnected widgets (sleep scores, heart rate spikes, steps, random badges) without providing actionable training guidance.
2. **Gimmicky, Disconnected AI:** AI assistants often feel like detached novelty chatbots lacking real-time awareness of the user’s recovery state, prior volume loads, and macro deltas.
3. **Rigid Workout Routines:** Static spreadsheet-style apps lead to trainee burnout when unexpected fatigue or time constraints occur.
4. **Distracting Commercialization:** Flashing discount banners, countdown timers, and neon gamification erode the serious, focused mindset lifters cultivate on the gym floor.

### 2.2 Primary Persona: "The Methodical Lifter" (Alex, 28)
- **Profile:** Trains 4–5 days/week with barbells, dumbbells, cables, and bodyweight; tracks caloric intake and macronutrient splits (protein/carbs/fat).
- **Core Goals:** Consistent progressive overload, balanced recovery, and clean macro adherence without spending 20 minutes manually fiddling with mobile inputs.
- **Key Frustrations:** Cluttered UIs, high cognitive friction before a lift, and assistants that feel like gimmicks.
- **Aesthetic Preference:** Dark obsidian interfaces, high-contrast typography, matte textures, and calm visual feedback.

## 3. Global Information Architecture & Navigation Modes

Saman enforces a **tri-mode navigation architecture**:
1. **Persistent 5-Tab Navigation System** across general exploration, coaching, nutrition, and profile hubs.
2. **Dedicated In-Session Operational Mode** during active training, live camera tracking, and initial onboarding setup, deliberately suppressing the global bottom tab bar to prevent accidental exits or destructive context switching.
3. **Focused Sub-Flow / Sub-Page Mode** (e.g., Edit Profile, Membership Overview, Compare All Plans, Plan Details) utilizing a dedicated safe-area top app bar with explicit back routing, omitting the global 5-tab bar to maintain task focus.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Viewport Top: Safe-Area Context Header / Subpage Back Arrow |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Dynamic Scrollable Viewport (Smooth Inertial Scrolling)     |
|   - Minimum Content clearance: pb-28 / pb-32                |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
| Fixed Bottom Navigation Bar (Glass Obsidian #0C0D0E / 95%)  |
| [1. Home] [2. Workout*] [3. Saman (S)] [4. Nutrition] [5. Profile] |
+-------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Top-Level Navigation Tabs
1. **Home**: Daily holistic summary (readiness telemetry, nutrition overview, adherence streak).
2. **Workout [Active Operational Core]**: Training launchpad, scheduled hero session, quick-start matrix, movement-focus exploration, forward horizon, and verified history.
3. **Saman Coach (Monogram `S`)**: Multimodal conversational AI coach providing workout and nutrition adjustments, RPE calibrations, and telemetry analysis.
4. **Nutrition**: Macronutrient tracking (protein, carbs, fats), meal logging, and hydration telemetry.
5. **Profile [Core Hub]**: User biometric telemetry, primary goals, activity level, connected apps, membership preview entry, and pre-CP0 fallback contracts.

## 4. Complete Screen Specifications & Design Contracts

### 4.1 Onboarding Flow (Steps 1–5)
- **Step 1: "Choose Your Goal"**:
  - 5-segment progress bar (Segment 1 active).
  - Editorial header with dark gym documentary photography.
  - Three photographic cards (120px height, 16px radius): *Lose weight*, *Maintain weight*, *Build muscle* with seamless right 35–40% photo blend.
  - Disabled Continue button until an explicit selection is made.
- **Step 2: "A little about you" (Age & Gender)**:
  - Direct numeric Age entry panel (30–32px tabular digits, `YEARS` label, `Enter age` placeholder).
  - Side-by-side equal-width Gender selection cards (*Male* / *Female*).
  - Provisional CP0 verification note for enums and age validation.
- **Step 3: "Your body measurements" (Height & Weight)**:
  - Stacked numeric input cards with lowercase non-wrapping `cm` and `kg` labels.
  - Supports exact decimal precision (e.g., `175.5 cm`, `72.5 kg`).
  - Zero premature BMI or body-fat predictions.
- **Step 4: "Activity Level"**:
  - 5 ergonomic tiers (*Mostly sitting*, *Lightly active*, *Moderately active*, *Very active*, *Extremely active*).
  - Neutrally weighted without gamified "Recommended" tags.
  - Enforces `pb-28` clearance ensuring the 5th card is accessible above the docked CTA.
- **Step 5: "Review & Confirm"**:
  - Four grouped cards displaying entered telemetry with dedicated `Edit` triggers (44×44px touch areas).
  - Tapping Edit returns to the exact step with draft preserved; finishing routes back to Step 5.
  - Backend Gate Contract: Requires confirmed profile save and backend readback confirming all 6 fields (`goal`, `age`, `gender`, `height`, `weight`, `activity level`).
  - External save state machine: *Saving (in-flight lock)*, *Save Failed (retry)*, and *Verification Timeout (readback retry)*.

---

### 4.2 Profile Tab & Pre-CP0 Architecture
- **Header**: Single-line title `Profile` (`Space Grotesk`, 30px bold) with blended documentary gym photo in top-right. Zero step counters or back chevrons.
- **Identity Card**: Display-only circular initials avatar (`NA` / `AM`), member name, email, and Free plan badge.
- **Friendly Profile Summary**: Cohesive charcoal container (`#16171B`, 16px radius) displaying Goal card, Age (`25 years`), Gender (`Male`), Height (`175.5 cm`), Weight (`72.5 kg`), and Activity Level (`Moderately active`). No inline form fields or sliders.
- **Action Triggers**: Full-width 54px `Edit profile` CTA leading into Milestone 5 mutation flow.
- **Account & Support**: Manage membership, Privacy & security, Export data log, Help & feedback, and `Log out`.

---

### 4.3 Edit Profile Mutation Flow (Milestone 5)
- **Container Architecture**:
  1. *Fixed Header*: Safe-area top bar, back chevron, title `Edit profile`, and subtitle with photo blend.
  2. *Scrollable Form (`flex-1`)*: Clips strictly above the Save dock; contains all 6 editable fields.
  3. *Static Save Dock (`shrink-0`)*: Sits outside scrollable container; houses full-width 54px `Save changes` button and home indicator.
- **Sub-States & Sheets**:
  - Goal Selection bottom sheet (reusing Step 1 photographic assets).
  - Activity Level bottom sheet (reusing Step 4 tiers).
  - Discard Confirmation dialog when navigating back with a dirty draft.
  - Keypad accessory toolbar with `Done` action.
- **Save State Machine**: Single-flight lock during persistence, 100% draft preservation on failure, and idempotent readback retry.

| **Metabolic Violet**| `#A855F7` | Recovery status, fat macronutrients |

### 5.2 Typographic Hierarchy
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Screen titles (24–30px), plan titles, card headlines, and primary metric figures.
- **Body & Interface (`Inter` / `SF Pro`)**: UI labels, body text, form cues, table rows, and benefit bullets. Monospace typography is strictly excluded from client-facing input fields.
- **Telemetry & Numbers (`JetBrains Mono` / Tabular Sans)**: Strict tabular numerals (`tabular-nums`) for prices, in-session chronometers, set tallies, calendar grids, and charts.

### 5.3 Elevation & Contour Philosophy
- **Zero Ambient Drop Shadows**: Depth achieved strictly via tonal stratification and 1px hairline borders.
- **Machined Radii**: 12px (`rounded-xl`) for buttons and small controls; 16px (`rounded-2xl`) for cards, panels, and modal containers.

## 6. Mathematical Data Honesty & Audit Rules

1. **Volume Calculation**: External Load $\times$ Completed Reps. Bodyweight exercises do not contribute to lifted volume.
2. **Timed Sets**: Contribute to set count and total timed work; excluded from rep totals and weight volume tallies.
3. **Cross-Screen Audit Agreement**:
   - Oct 10: Dumbbell Bench Press ($24\text{ kg} \times 8 = 192\text{ kg}$).
   - Oct 8: Upper Body Strength ($656\text{ kg} + 760\text{ kg} + 420\text{ kg} = 1,836\text{ kg}$).
   - Oct 6: Lower Body & Core ($480\text{ kg} + 570\text{ kg} = 1,050\text{ kg}$ + $1\text{m } 30\text{s}$ plank).
   - Monthly Overview: $192 + 1,836 + 1,050 = \mathbf{3,078\text{ kg}}$ across $3\text{ workouts}$, $14\text{ sets}$, and $1\text{h } 32\text{m}$.
4. **Membership Annual Pricing Math**:
   - Plus: $\$60 / 12 = \mathbf{\$5 / \text{month}}$ equivalent.
   - Pro: $\$120 / 12 = \mathbf{\$10 / \text{month}}$ equivalent.
   - Ultra: $\$240 / 12 = \mathbf{\$20 / \text{month}}$ equivalent.
